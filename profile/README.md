# 🏛️ Council of AI (CSOAI)

**Independent AI-behaviour measurement. Open source. Bitcoin-anchored.**

## What We Do

CSOAI measures AI systems across 33 governance axes (23 measured, 10 unmeasured), publishes signed evidence cards, and anchors proof to Bitcoin via Merkle trees. Every measurement is cryptographically verifiable.
