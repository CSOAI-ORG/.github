## The wedge (why inbound comes to us, free)
Article 50(2) is enforceable now and **requires a free detection service** with
**unrestricted access to authorities, media, fact-checkers, researchers, and civil
society.** We ship exactly that (`/api/detect` — signed, neutral, cross-lab). The
law itself is the distribution channel: those constituencies are sent to a free
verifier, and ours is the signed, open one. That is top-of-funnel we don't pay for.

```
Article 50 free detection (/api/detect, free)      ← the law sends researchers/press/regulators
  → they cite the signed verdict / interop suite    ← credibility + backlinks

      → paid readiness work (NOT a stamp)             ← revenue funds the instrument
```
