# Code of Conduct

Council of AI (CSOAI Ltd) is a public measurement body. We work in the
open. We expect the same standard of conduct we apply to our own board:
honest, auditable, and not for sale.

## Our standard

- Challenge measurements with evidence. Do not demand a rank, a grade, or a faster verdict.
- Do not harass, impersonate, or dox anyone.
- Do not submit content that is illegal, sexually exploitative, or that involves minors.
- Do not use this account’s repositories to run scams, malware, or undisclosed surveillance.
- Do not present a measurement as a certification, a licence, or a regulator decision.

## Enforcement

Report conduct that fails this standard to [nicholas@csoai.org](mailto:nicholas@csoai.org).
We will review reports and may remove content, close issues, or block accounts.

This code applies to GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, and any
space we host that points here.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
