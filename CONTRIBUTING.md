# Contributing

CSOAI Ltd measures AI systems against published rules and signs the result.
We do not remediate, certify, or sell a grade.

## What we accept

- **Recompute challenges.** A signed receipt you can reproduce from a public
  harness, with the split, grader, and disagreement stated.
- **Corrections of fact.** Wrong URLs, stale dates, or a claim that
  contradicts the living board at [councilof.ai/api/gspc](https://councilof.ai/api/gspc).
- **Tooling patches** to the named open instruments:
  [carder](https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/carder),
  [inspect-receipts](https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/inspect-receipts),
  [a2a-signed-receipts](https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/a2a-signed-receipts),
  [codabench-gspc](https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/codabench-gspc).

## What we do not accept

- Requests to change a rank, hide a loss, or expedite a verdict.
- New MCP servers, marketplace listings, or “hive” domain stubs.
- Marketing copy, pricing pages, or personal/unrelated projects.
- Unsigned numbers presented as board results.

## How to send work

1. Open an issue with the template that matches the work (challenge or defect).
2. Fork the relevant repository and open a pull request against `main`.
3. Keep the change small. Point at the living API or a signed receipt; do not hardcode counts.

Sponsorship funds compute and re-measurement. It never buys a result.
See the [Firewall Charter](https://councilof.ai/firewall-charter).
